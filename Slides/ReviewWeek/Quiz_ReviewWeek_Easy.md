# Review Week Quiz — Easy

20 multiple-choice questions + 1 open-ended feedback question. Each question tests one idea, and all calculations use small whole numbers. Correct answers marked ✓ — remove before giving to students.

## Measurement scales

**Q1.** Eye color (brown, blue, green) is measured on which scale?
**A) Nominal ✓**  B) Ordinal  C) Interval  D) Ratio

**Q2.** Finishing place in a race (1st, 2nd, 3rd) is measured on which scale?
A) Nominal  **B) Ordinal ✓**  C) Interval  D) Ratio

**Q3.** Which scale of measurement has a true zero point (zero means "none")?
A) Nominal  B) Ordinal  C) Interval  **D) Ratio ✓**

## Mean, median, and mode

**Q4.** What is the mean of 2, 4, 6, 8?
A) 4  B) 6  **C) 5 ✓**  D) 20

**Q5.** What is the median of 1, 3, 5, 7, 9?
A) 3  **B) 5 ✓**  C) 7  D) 25

**Q6.** What is the mode of 2, 3, 3, 3, 6, 8?
**A) 3 ✓**  B) 4  C) 6  D) 8

## Positive and negative skew

**Q7.** In a symmetrical distribution, the mean, median, and mode are:
A) Mean is the largest  B) Mode is the largest  **C) About equal ✓**  D) Median is the smallest

**Q8.** In a negatively skewed distribution, the tail points toward:
A) The right (high scores)  B) Both sides equally  C) There is no tail  **D) The left (low scores) ✓**

**Q9.** For the scores 2, 7, 8, 9, 9: mean = 7, median = 8, mode = 9. The distribution is:
A) Positively skewed  **B) Negatively skewed ✓**  C) Symmetrical  D) Bimodal

## Standard deviation

**Q10.** What is the standard deviation of the scores 5, 5, 5, 5?
**A) 0 ✓**  B) 1  C) 5  D) 20

**Q11.** A larger standard deviation means the scores are:
A) Closer together  B) All the same  **C) More spread out ✓**  D) Higher on average

**Q12.** If the variance is 9, what is the standard deviation?
A) 81  B) 4.5  C) 9  **D) 3 ✓**

## Normal distribution characteristics

**Q13.** The normal distribution is:

**Q14.** In a normal distribution, what percentage of scores falls above the mean?

**Q15.** Test scores are normally distributed with mean 50 and SD 10. About 68% of students score between:

## Standard normal distribution

**Q17.** When every score is converted to a z-score, the new distribution has:

## z-scores

**Q18.** A score that is exactly equal to the mean has a z-score of:
A) 1  B) −1  **C) 0 ✓**  D) It cannot be determined

**Q19.** A test has mean 60 and SD 10. A student scores 80. What is the z-score?
**A) 2 ✓**  B) 20  C) 0.5  D) 8

**Q20.** A z-score of −1.0 means the score is:
A) 1 point below the mean  B) Above the mean  C) Exactly at the mean  **D) One standard deviation below the mean ✓**

Worked calculations:
- Q4: (2 + 4 + 6 + 8) / 4 = 20 / 4 = 5
- Q5: middle (3rd) of 5 ordered scores = 5
- Q6: 3 appears three times
- Q9: 35 / 5 = 7; mean < median < mode → negative skew
- Q10: every score equals the mean (5), so all deviations are 0
- Q12: SD = √9 = 3
- Q15: 50 ± 10 → 40 to 60
- Q19: z = (80 − 60) / 10 = 2
