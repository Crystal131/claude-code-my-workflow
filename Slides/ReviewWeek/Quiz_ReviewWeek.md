**Worked solutions for the calculation items**

- **Q4:** Mean = (2 + 4 + 4 + 5 + 10) / 5 = 25 / 5 = 5; median = middle (3rd) score = 4; mode = 4 (appears twice).
- **Q5:** *n* = 6 (even), so average the 3rd and 4th scores: (8 + 12) / 2 = 10. (D, 10.83, is the mean.)
- **Q11:** Mean = 4; deviations −2, 0, 2; SS = 4 + 0 + 4 = 8; s² = 8 / (3 − 1) = 4; s = √4 = 2. (A, 1.63, is the population SD, √(8/3).)
- **Q15:** 100 ± 2(15) = 70 to 130.
- **Q18:** z = (82 − 70) / 8 = 12 / 8 = 1.50.
- **Q19:** X = μ + zσ = 50 + (−2)(5) = 40.
- **Q20:** Math z = (88 − 80) / 4 = 2.0; English z = (90 − 85) / 10 = 0.5.
